A pharmacy store, showcased shelf by shelf

12 minute phone capture - Showcases the product shelf by shelf - Geometrically accurate.

Jai Tyagi · 9 October 2026 · 4 min read

For MSMEs, the aim was to provide a structurally coherent representation of their inventory and operations with a floor plan; the procedure and methodology followed was the same as that for the house model.

A quick breakdown of the pipeline:

  1. 1
    Data capture

    Capture a series of images and videos of the shop on a smartphone by walking along a planned path.

  2. 2
    Structure-from-Motion (SfM)

    The captured footage is processed with photogrammetry software, which analyzes the images and extracts key features to build a 3D point cloud.

  3. 3
    3D reconstruction

    The point cloud is used to build a detailed 3D model of the shop, reconstructing its geometry and textures into a realistic representation.

  4. 4
    Interactive visualization

    Finally, the model is prepared for interactive viewing, so anyone can explore it from any angle.

Shop Structure

Here the processed SfM point cloud is used to generate a geometric representation of the house's layout. The orange frames are called view frustums that depict where the camera was positioned during the capture. As such, they provide context for understanding the 3D model's perspective. A top view or an orthophoto of this layout helps visualize the space and floor plan more effectively.

Stock and Interior

The stock and interior layout of the store can be effectively audited by visualizing the gaussian model resulting from the point cloud's processing. Here, the capture method was same as that of the house model, a good structural coherence can be observed here with decent detail of the stock, this quality however can be further improved with more photos taken from different angles.
